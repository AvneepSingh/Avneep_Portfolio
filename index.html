A little about me.

I'm a computer science graduate student based in Montréal, Canada. My interests span machine learning, human-centred software, information retrieval, and the systems that make modern technology dependable.

Before joining Concordia University, I studied Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Guwahati. Along the way, I've worked in software development, supported students as a teaching assistant and tutor, and explored projects ranging from document intelligence to generative models.
