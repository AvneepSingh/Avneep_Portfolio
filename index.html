01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

I'm a computer science graduate student based in Montréal, Canada. My interests span machine learning, human-centred software, information retrieval, and the systems that make modern technology dependable.

I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at IIIT Guwahati. My research spans CNN ensembles and hybrid CNN–Transformers at Panjab University, GAN-based low-light image translation at Punjab Engineering College, and applied computer vision. Alongside research, I build full-stack applications, teach, and mentor future computer science students through ByteBoost.
